# InkZero AR

The AR part of InkZero. Everything about it is in the [root README](../README.md).

- `docs/`: the proposal, its figures, the UML diagrams, the naming index, the scene format, related work.
- `scenes/`: scene files, one per scene.
- `tools/`: small scripts that are not the app.
- `unity/`: the Unity project, from Sprint 1.
